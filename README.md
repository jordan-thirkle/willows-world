# Willow's World

**Their childhood goes faster than your camera roll can keep up.**

Willow's World is a private, long-lived childhood archive designed to preserve not only photographs, but the stories around them: what was happening, when it happened, who was there, and what the moment meant.

The product is being built first for Willow, with a wider vision of helping parents deliberately document a child's childhood instead of leaving it buried in a camera roll.

## Product principles

- **Child first.** The archive exists for the child as much as the parent.
- **Private by default.** Childhood media and identity data are sensitive and must never become public accidentally.
- **Photos + stories.** Preserve context, not just image files.
- **Parent's voice.** AI may help draft memories, but the finished writing should sound personal rather than synthetic.
- **Timeline over camera roll.** Moments should become a coherent story that can be revisited tonight, next year, or decades later.
- **Durable originals.** Original media must be safely stored, exportable, and recoverable.
- **No AI slop.** Calm, timeless, editorial design over generic AI-dashboard aesthetics.

## Initial product direction

1. Import photos and videos.
2. Identify likely photos containing the child, with explicit parent control over identity/face features.
3. Understand useful context such as date, people, place and activity.
4. Group related media into moments.
5. Draft a short memory in the parent's voice.
6. Let the parent review, edit and approve it.
7. Present approved memories on a beautiful chronological timeline.
8. Make the archive easy to browse together now and eventually hand over to the child.
