# Willow's World — Anti-Scope-Creep Rules

Until P0 is verified, default to **no** for work whose primary purpose is:

- adding another world destination;
- building a broad mini-game catalogue;
- social graph/DM/community features;
- subscriptions or complex monetization;
- large account/admin systems beyond what private data actually requires;
- speculative microservices or provider abstractions with no proven caller;
- SEO page volume before a strong public information architecture exists;
- visual redesigns that do not improve the founding Story Woods experience.

An exception requires evidence that the work removes a material safety/release blocker or is necessary for the current vertical slice.

## Reference-project rule

Other By JTT projects can contribute standards, lessons and reusable patterns. They do not silently contribute product requirements. Willow's World scope comes from its own product documents and approved decisions.
