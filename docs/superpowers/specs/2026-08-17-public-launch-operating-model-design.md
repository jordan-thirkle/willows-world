# Willow's World — Public Launch Operating Model Design

## Decision

Willow's World advances through four evidence-gated product stages while preserving the founding loop **Record → Transform → Watch / Play → Share** as the north star.

The separate childhood archive project is a reference for By JTT product discipline only. It is not a Willow's World dependency and its product scope must not leak into this project.

## Gate P0 — Product-complete alpha

Episode 001 proves the complete founding loop using one canonical `StoryPerformance` revision.

Exit evidence:

1. A real recording can enter the private production workflow without being committed to GitHub or exposed publicly.
2. Source media is preserved immutably and derivatives are non-destructive.
3. Transcript and word timing are reviewable and parent-approved.
4. A canonical ScenePlan and approved art assets drive Story Woods.
5. Story Woods is genuinely playable with synchronized real voice, words, scenes and deterministic timing.
6. Completion emits explicit learning/progression events and visibly changes Willow's World.
7. Restarting the application preserves approved performance and progression state.
8. Remotion renders landscape and vertical masters from the same approved performance and scene plan.
9. Parent preview/approval gates every export/publication path.
10. Web/mobile interaction, accessibility, sync and failure states are verified with evidence.

P0 does not require broad accounts, subscriptions, social features, extra destinations or speculative mini-games.

## Gate P1 — Distribution-complete beta

The proven product becomes a real cross-platform application rather than a web demonstration.

Exit evidence:

- Expo/React Native is the primary family client on iOS and Android.
- iOS and Android development/release builds install and complete the P0 loop on real/simulator devices as appropriate.
- Durable database/object-storage adapters replace fixture-only persistence for private production data.
- Authentication/parent boundary exists where remote private data requires identity.
- Export and deletion pathways exist for private family data before external family beta.
- Crash/error observability is configured without behavioural advertising or invasive child analytics.
- Accessibility, offline/interrupted-media behaviour, media permissions, recovery and low-connectivity paths are tested.
- Production web and mobile clients consume compatible versioned domain records.
- CI/release verification can reproduce build/test/typecheck/lint gates without depending on one developer machine.

## Gate P2 — Public-launch complete

Willow's World can safely be offered publicly and submitted to the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Exit evidence:

- Public and private trust zones are technically separated.
- Public publishing requires an explicit parent-approved publication record.
- Launch content has documented publication rights; internal Episode 001 material with third-party rights constraints is not silently treated as publishable IP.
- Privacy policy, terms/support surfaces, deletion/export instructions and contact routes are live.
- App Store and Play Store metadata, screenshots, icons, age/content declarations and privacy/data-safety disclosures are prepared from actual product behaviour.
- Production builds pass platform review-readiness checks and contain no development secrets/test endpoints/private child fixtures.
- Production web is deployed from GitHub with a durable By JTT-owned domain/subdomain strategy.
- Monitoring, rollback/recovery and release notes exist.
- A launch checklist records evidence rather than relying on an agent's assertion that the app is ready.

## Gate P3 — Organic-growth launch

The product has a deliberate public discovery system without compromising the child/family experience.

Exit evidence:

- A high-quality public Willow's World landing surface explains the product and routes into appropriate By JTT properties.
- Public web pages are crawlable and have intentional titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, structured data where valid, sitemap and robots behaviour.
- Search Console and privacy-safe analytics are connected for the public web surface.
- Original/publication-safe episode or editorial pages can become durable search destinations instead of forcing SEO copy into the child-facing app.
- One approved performance can feed an intentional YouTube landscape and short-form distribution workflow without manually rebuilding the episode.
- X.com marketing uses authored launch/progress/storytelling material rather than automated spam.
- Growth measurement focuses on qualified discovery, conversion and production-loop success; child time-in-app is not a growth KPI.

## Professional operating hierarchy

Decision order for every material task:

1. **Child safety, privacy, rights and source preservation** — cannot be traded away for speed or growth.
2. **Product truth** — preserve real voice/performance and parent-approved facts; generated interpretations remain replaceable.
3. **Founding-loop quality** — work must improve or unblock Record → Transform → Watch / Play → Share until P0 passes.
4. **Reliability and verification** — tests, device/browser evidence, reproducibility, rollback and observability precede claims of completion.
5. **Distribution readiness** — native platform quality and public/private separation precede store/public launch.
6. **Discoverability and growth** — SEO/social/distribution amplify a product that already works; they do not substitute for it.
7. **Expansion** — Maker Meadow, Discovery Hill, broader games/social/monetization earn priority only after preceding gates are healthy.

## Agent operating model

- GitHub is the durable source of truth for code, safe fixtures, specifications, plans and verification records.
- Private child media, credentials and secrets never enter the public repository.
- Meaningful changes use a branch/PR/review/verification path.
- Work is organized as vertical slices that leave independently testable software, not long architecture-only phases.
- Every blocker follows: detect → reproduce → root cause → alternatives → durable fix → verify → document → resume original goal.
- Agents may research and challenge assumptions, but must distinguish repository evidence, external evidence and inference.
- No completion claim without fresh verification evidence.
- Meta-work has a budget: documentation/process changes must unlock implementation, safety, verification or continuity rather than becoming the project.
- New features are rejected when they do not materially advance the current gate.

## Release progression

`P0 product proof → P1 native/private beta → P2 public/store launch → P3 organic growth → post-launch expansion`

A later gate may be prepared in parallel only when it does not distract from an unmet earlier gate. For example, domain/SEO architecture can be designed during P0, but it must not displace completion of Episode 001.

## End state

Willow's World is complete enough to launch when a parent can privately capture a real reading moment, review what the system derived, let the child experience that moment as a polished interactive world, preserve meaningful learning/progression, create approved media outputs from the same source, and deliberately publish only safe/rightful derivatives — with the experience working on web, iOS and Android and supported by a professional By JTT public presence.
