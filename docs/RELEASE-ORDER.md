# Willow's World — Release Order

## Critical path

1. Prove one canonical Episode 001 contract.
2. Prove deterministic Story Woods playback/progression.
3. Prove parent review/approval and one-performance/many-output rendering.
4. Make canonical/private state durable.
5. Converge proven experience into Expo and verify iOS/Android.
6. Add the minimum family identity/export/delete controls required for private beta.
7. Build explicit public projection and rights-safe public content.
8. Produce verified store/web release candidates.
9. Launch public discovery/distribution system.
10. Expand the world based on evidence.

## Safe parallel preparation

During P0, visual continuity work, rights-safe original content planning, release metadata research and public information architecture may proceed only when they do not displace the core implementation path.

During P1, public web/store asset preparation may proceed while native QA runs, but P2 cannot be declared complete until P1 evidence passes.

## Forbidden shortcut

Do not launch marketing around a prototype and call that product launch. Do not submit wrapper/native shells that do not genuinely implement the verified family experience. Do not create public episode pages from private material without approval/rights eligibility.
