# Willow's World — P3 Acceptance Contract

P3 proves the public product can be discovered and understood organically without compromising the private child/family experience.

## Required evidence

- production landing/public content routes render meaningful indexable content;
- canonical/title/description/share metadata, sitemap and robots behavior are verified;
- structured data is used only where valid for visible content;
- Search Console and privacy-safe analytics are connected to the production public property;
- approved landscape and vertical media outputs have traceable distribution metadata;
- X/public launch material is authored and privacy-safe;
- measurement distinguishes qualified discovery/conversion from vanity traffic;
- child time-in-app is not used as a growth optimization target.

P3 does not require mass programmatic SEO pages, automated social spam or exposing private childhood material for engagement.
