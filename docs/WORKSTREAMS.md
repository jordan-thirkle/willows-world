# Willow's World — Workstreams

These are delivery perspectives, not permission to run unrelated scope.

## Product and Creative
Owns north-star fidelity, parent/child ritual, world coherence and release-gate prioritization.

## Story Production
Owns ingest, transcript/timing review, scene planning, art continuity, audio treatment and one-performance/many-output integrity.

## Family App
Owns Expo iOS/Android child/parent experience, media lifecycle, accessibility, interruption behavior and native quality.

## Public Web and Growth
Owns discoverable public product/editorial surfaces, technical SEO, By JTT relationship and approved distribution metadata.

## Platform and Data
Owns canonical domain records, persistence, object storage, versioning, portability, production jobs and provider boundaries.

## Privacy and Rights
Owns private/public separation, authorization, publication approval, provenance/rights eligibility, data minimization and export/deletion behavior.

## QA and Release
Owns the evidence matrix, regression/device/browser/accessibility testing, release-candidate verification, store readiness, monitoring and rollback.

## Operating rule
Parallelize only genuinely independent work. The earliest incomplete gate controls priority. Each workstream leaves evidence and a durable handoff rather than relying on chat context.
