# Willow's World — Product Expansion Gate

Maker Meadow, Discovery Hill, additional games and other destinations are not rejected ideas. They are gated ideas.

Before materially expanding the world, require:

- defining Story Woods loop is verified and stable;
- native/private foundation is not being bypassed;
- expansion has a clear relationship to real learning/creativity/family moments;
- data/privacy/rights implications are understood;
- the experience adds distinctive depth rather than generic activity count;
- capacity exists without degrading release reliability or public launch work.

Post-launch evidence can then determine which destination deserves the next vertical slice.
