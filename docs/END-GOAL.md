# Willow's World — End Goal

Willow's World is heading toward a public, production-quality family product — not an endless prototype.

A parent should be able to capture/import a real child's reading, preserve it safely, review/correct what AI-assisted production derives, and turn the approved performance into a polished interactive illustrated world. The child's real voice drives the experience; learning/progression persists; the same approved performance produces landscape and vertical media; publication is deliberate, rights-aware and parent-controlled.

The product must be genuinely usable on iOS and Android, supported by a professional public web presence under By JTT, ready for App Store and Play Store distribution, operable/recoverable in production, and discoverable through responsible organic search/media/social distribution.

Future world destinations may expand this foundation, but they are not allowed to replace it. The project is complete enough to launch when P0, P1 and P2 evidence gates pass; P3 establishes the organic launch/growth system around that verified product.
