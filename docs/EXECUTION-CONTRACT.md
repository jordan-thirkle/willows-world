# Willow's World — Execution Contract

When given permission to proceed autonomously, continue through multiple safe implementation/review/verification slices rather than stopping after the first small success.

## Continue while

- the next action is clearly supported by approved product/architecture decisions;
- required tools/permissions are available;
- the action is reversible or follows the established PR/release process;
- evidence can be gathered honestly;
- no genuine product/safety/rights decision requires the project owner's judgment.

## Stop and ask when

- two materially different product directions remain and the approved documents do not decide between them;
- an irreversible/public/account-level action requires explicit confirmation;
- required private information/credential/account action cannot be safely inferred;
- evidence shows the approved design itself is materially wrong and changing it would alter the product promise.

## Do not stop merely because

- one task/PR completed;
- a preferred tool failed but viable alternatives exist;
- documentation could be improved further after the meta-work budget is satisfied;
- a later safe task is available in the current gate.

Autonomy never overrides evidence, privacy, rights or explicit confirmation requirements for irreversible external actions.
