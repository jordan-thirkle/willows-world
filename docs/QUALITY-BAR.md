# Willow's World — Quality Bar

Willow's World should be judged as a real children's/family product, not on whether an AI agent produced a large amount of code.

## Experience

- Story Woods feels like entering an authored illustrated world rather than navigating an education dashboard.
- Real voice/performance remains emotionally central.
- Motion supports reading and atmosphere instead of competing for attention.
- Parent controls are understandable without leaking production complexity into the child's experience.
- Phone use is first-class; desktop is not the hidden design baseline.

## Visual craft

- warm tactile illustration, coherent typography and layered scenery;
- consistent Willow/world continuity across interactive and rendered media;
- no default neon gradients, glass-card SaaS language or generic AI-generated visual clutter;
- generated assets require continuity/quality review rather than being accepted because generation succeeded.

## Learning

Progression should represent meaningful reading/word encounters and be reconstructable from explicit events. Avoid manipulative streaks, arbitrary currencies and engagement mechanics unrelated to the learning/family ritual.

## Technical craft

- one canonical approved performance drives all delivery formats;
- derived work is reproducible/versioned;
- source material is preserved;
- failure/retry does not destroy earlier approved work;
- private/public boundaries are explicit;
- verification matches the platform being claimed.

## Launch quality

A polished landing page cannot compensate for a weak Story Woods experience. Likewise, a good prototype cannot compensate for unsafe storage or unverifiable native builds. The release gate evaluates the whole relevant stage, not the strongest screenshot.
