# Willow's World — Product Gates

| Gate | User-facing outcome | Must be true before exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 Product-complete alpha | A real reading becomes a polished playable/shareable Willow's World experience | complete founding loop, persistent progression, dual-format render, parent approval, verification |
| P1 Distribution-complete beta | The family experience works as a durable private iOS/Android product | native clients, durable private persistence, authorization, export/delete, recovery and reproducible builds |
| P2 Public-launch complete | The product can safely be distributed publicly and submitted to stores | rights-safe content, public/private boundary, support/privacy, verified release candidates, monitoring/rollback |
| P3 Organic-growth launch | Families can discover the product through a professional By JTT public presence | crawlable useful web, technical SEO, analytics/Search Console, approved media and authored social distribution |

The gates are sequential for completion, not necessarily for all preparation. Earlier unmet gates always win priority.
