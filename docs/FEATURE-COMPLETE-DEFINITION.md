# Willow's World — Definition of Feature Complete

`Feature complete` is stage-specific. It must not be used as a vague synonym for "a lot has been built."

## P0 feature complete

The founding product works end-to-end for Episode 001: real/private ingest, reviewable timed performance, approved scene plan/assets, synchronized Story Woods, persistent progression, landscape/vertical outputs, parent approval and verified web/mobile behavior.

## P1 feature complete

The P0 experience is a durable private beta on real iOS/Android clients with production-suitable persistence, authorization, family-data export/deletion, recovery behavior, observability and reproducible builds.

## P2 feature complete / launch ready

The product can be responsibly offered to the public: publication trust boundary, rights-safe launch content, privacy/support surfaces, verified store release candidates, accurate platform disclosures, production web, monitoring and rollback all pass evidence gates.

## P3 growth complete for launch

The public product has an intentional organic discovery system: technically sound crawlable web, analytics/Search Console, approved media distribution and By JTT social launch operations with measurable qualified discovery.

## Not required for initial public launch

Unless evidence later changes priority, initial launch does not require broad social networking, child DMs, behavioural advertising, subscriptions, a large mini-game catalogue, Maker Meadow, Discovery Hill or every imagined world destination.

Those can be valuable later. They are not allowed to obscure whether the defining Willow's World experience is excellent and safe.
