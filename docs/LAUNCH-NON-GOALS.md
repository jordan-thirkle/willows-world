# Willow's World — Initial Launch Non-goals

The initial public release does not need to prove every possible future business/product idea.

Unless later evidence changes priority, launch does not require:

- public child profiles, DMs or social graph;
- behavioural advertising;
- subscriptions/complex billing;
- a broad multiplayer/community system;
- every future world destination;
- a large catalogue of mini-games;
- massive programmatic SEO;
- fully automated social publishing;
- provider-specific architecture that makes migration difficult.

The launch must instead prove a deep, safe, polished defining experience and professional distribution/operations.
