# Willow's World — Success Criteria

## Product
A parent can complete the founding loop with a real performance and understand/control every approval/publication step.

## Child experience
Story Woods is delightful, legible, responsive and synchronized on a phone, and progression meaningfully reflects reading encounters without manipulative engagement mechanics.

## Preservation
Canonical source/approved records can be exported/reconstructed independently of today's UI/AI provider.

## Distribution
Verified iOS and Android applications plus a professional production public web presence exist.

## Safety/rights
Private family data cannot accidentally cross into public output; public launch content has explicit publication eligibility.

## Operations
A release candidate can be built, verified, deployed, monitored and rolled back/recovered using documented repeatable processes.

## Growth
Relevant families can discover and understand the product through useful public search/media/social surfaces, with qualified conversion measurable without optimizing child screen time.
