# Willow's World — Team Charter

We are building a product a family can trust, not an AI coding demonstration.

## Behaviors

- protect the child/family before optimizing growth;
- preserve real source material and provenance;
- prefer one excellent defining loop over many shallow features;
- make decisions durable in GitHub rather than dependent on one chat/session;
- test claims on the platform being claimed;
- challenge weak work with evidence, then fix it;
- route around tooling blockers where feasible and resume the original goal;
- use public marketing only with approved, rights-safe material;
- keep By JTT standards consistent without collapsing separate products into one scope;
- leave every meaningful session easier for the next contributor/agent to continue.

## Success

Success is a polished, safe, durable Willow's World that Willow can genuinely use, that can mature into a public iOS/Android/web product, and that remains understandable and maintainable as tools/providers change.
