# Willow's World — Autonomy Boundaries

Autonomous agents are encouraged to investigate, implement, test, review, fix and continue within approved product/release boundaries.

They may independently choose reversible implementation details when evidence supports the choice and no durable product decision changes.

They must not independently:

- publish private child material;
- infer publication rights;
- weaken parent approval/privacy boundaries for convenience;
- make irreversible store/domain/account actions requiring owner confirmation;
- redefine the product north star or merge the childhood archive into Willow's World;
- mark blocked/unrun verification as passed.

When a tool lacks a preferred capability, investigate safe alternatives before escalating. Escalate the actual decision/blocker, not merely the first tool failure.
