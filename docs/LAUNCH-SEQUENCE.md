# Willow's World — Launch Sequence

1. Verify P0 with private/internal Episode 001.
2. Verify P1 on iOS/Android with durable private family data controls.
3. Prepare/verify owned or otherwise publication-eligible launch content.
4. Verify public projection, support/privacy and production web.
5. Build and verify store release candidates.
6. Verify current Apple/Google declarations/metadata/assets against actual builds.
7. Create final launch evidence record.
8. Perform explicit production/store publication actions.
9. Verify post-release health.
10. Activate/measure P3 organic discovery system.

This sequence prevents marketing/store pressure from turning internal development material or unverified native shells into a premature public release.
