# Willow's World — Owner Decision Gate

Ask for project-owner input when the choice materially changes:

- product promise/audience;
- what private family material may be published;
- monetization/business model when it becomes launch-relevant;
- irreversible public/store/domain/account action;
- rights/permission decision that cannot be established from evidence;
- a major roadmap trade-off between two genuinely different product directions.

Do not ask the owner to choose routine reversible engineering details that the approved architecture/evidence can decide.
