## Automated verification

## Experience verification
Record browser/device/simulator flows, target viewports/platform versions and screenshots/video references where appropriate.

## Privacy/rights/publication
Record private/public leakage checks, authorization checks, rights eligibility for public launch content and parent-approval behavior.

## Media outputs
Record performance/scene/asset revision IDs and evidence for landscape/vertical/captions/thumbnails.

## Accessibility
Record checks performed and material findings/fixes.

## Production operations
Record monitoring/health, deployment result, rollback/recovery verification and known non-gating limitations.

## Store readiness
Record current Apple/Google checklist results and links/identifiers for metadata/assets/declarations when applicable.

## Verdict
Use one of: `blocked`, `implemented-not-verified`, `verified-for-gate`, `released`.

Do not fill this template with assumed/pass-by-default values.
