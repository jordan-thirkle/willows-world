# Willow's World — Rights and Provenance Questions

For content/art/audio/text destined for public output:

1. What is the source?
2. Who owns/controls it or what license/permission permits use?
3. Which destinations/territories/uses are permitted?
4. Is the evidence recorded rather than assumed?
5. Are AI-generated/edited derivatives traceable to source assets and generation/edit provenance?
6. Does a parent approval exist for the exact public revision?
7. Would changing the source/scene/render require re-review?
8. Is this content safe to use for marketing/store screenshots as well as in-app/private development?

When uncertain, keep the material private-development-only until eligibility is established.
