# Willow's World — X Distribution Guardrails

Use X.com as a public By JTT storytelling/distribution channel, not as a feed of private family life.

Good material includes:
- product craft and design decisions;
- approved public artwork/animations;
- technical milestones and lessons;
- rights-safe public episodes/clips;
- launch/release updates;
- thoughtful commentary on preserving real voice/learning moments with AI-assisted production.

Avoid:
- private recordings/details published for engagement;
- automated high-frequency spam;
- claims that a prototype is App Store/public ready without evidence;
- fabricated child quotes/memories;
- third-party story material whose publication rights are unresolved.

Marketing truth follows product truth.
