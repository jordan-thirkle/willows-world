# Willow's World — Product Principles

1. **The real child voice is the hero.** AI assists production; it does not manufacture a replacement childhood performance.
2. **One performance, many outputs.** Interactive, landscape and vertical experiences share canonical timing/scenes/assets.
3. **A world, not a dashboard.** Product structure should feel spatial, illustrated and authored.
4. **Parent controlled.** Review and approval precede export/publication.
5. **Private by default.** Family data does not become public through convenience or inference.
6. **Generated work is replaceable.** Preserve source material and approved corrections so future tools can regenerate derivatives.
7. **Mobile first, native quality.** Web is important, but it does not substitute for iOS/Android evidence.
8. **Learning without manipulation.** Progress can be delightful without streak pressure or engagement-at-any-cost design.
9. **Rights are explicit.** Renderable is not publishable.
10. **Depth before breadth.** Make Story Woods and the founding loop exceptional before expanding the world.
