# Willow's World — Launch Feasibility Critic Report — 2026-08-17

## Verdict
**Feasible, but not currently launch-ready. Do not restart the product.**

The existing product north star and architecture are coherent enough to reach a real web/iOS/Android public product. The principal risk is not lack of ideas; it is mistaking prototype/documentation progress for verified end-to-end product progress or allowing scope expansion to outrun the founding loop.

## Strong foundations
- distinctive real-voice parent/child production loop;
- one-performance/many-output architecture;
- explicit parent control and private/public separation direction;
- Expo + web + Remotion roles are complementary rather than redundant;
- provider-neutral/durable source philosophy is appropriate for a long-lived childhood/learning product.

## Material gaps before public launch
- canonical domain/player/progression contracts are not yet evidenced as complete;
- durable private persistence/authorization is not yet evidenced;
- native iOS/Android product behavior is not yet evidenced;
- public projection/store/legal/support/release operations are not yet evidenced;
- internal Episode 001 content rights must not be assumed to permit public distribution;
- organic discovery architecture is a later launch layer, not proof of product readiness.

## Decision
Proceed with the existing product. Freeze speculative breadth. Implement and verify P0 vertically, then converge into native/private beta before public/store launch.
