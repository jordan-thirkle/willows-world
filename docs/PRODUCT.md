# Willow's World — Product North Star

## Founding idea

A parent teaches a young child to read the books they already share at bedtime, records the child reading, and AI-assisted tools transform the real performance into an exceptionally polished, visually consistent illustrated/animated experience.

That same approved performance should become both an interactive story inside Willow's World and shareable video formats for YouTube and short-form platforms.

## Promise

**Turn real childhood learning moments into a world worth keeping.**

The magic is not AI generating a fictional childhood. The magic is taking something real — the child's voice, mistakes, pauses, confidence and improvement — and giving it professional creative production.

## P0 core loop

**Record → Transform → Watch / Play → Share**

1. Parent records/imports the child's real reading.
2. Preserve the original recording; process derivatives non-destructively.
3. Clean audio where useful without manufacturing a different performance.
4. Transcribe with reviewable word-level timing.
5. Parent corrects/approves transcript and timing.
6. A canonical scene plan and art bible drive consistent illustrations.
7. Story Woods plays the real voice while words/scenes respond in time.
8. Completion updates learned words and changes the world.
9. The same performance renders 16:9 and 9:16 video outputs.
10. Parent previews and approves export/publication.

## Episode 001

The existing real Gruffalo reading recording is the current development performance. A new recording is deliberately deferred until the pipeline is proven.

Publication rights and technical capability are tracked separately: the pipeline may use a recording for internal development without representing third-party text as original Willow's World IP.

## Character continuity

Canonical Willow representation:

- brown hair
- blue eyes
- purple glasses
- age-appropriate child proportions
- warm, expressive storybook performance
- consistent silhouette and identifying features across app, landscape video and vertical video

## One performance, many outputs

A single approved `StoryPerformance` is the source for:

- Story Woods interactive read-along
- learning/Word Garden state
- 1920×1080 landscape master
- 1080×1920 vertical master
- captions/subtitles
- poster/thumbnail derivatives

We do not manually rebuild the same episode for each platform.

## Product world

Reading is the emotional centre, not the eventual boundary.

- **Story Woods** — reading and interactive illustrated stories
- **Word Garden** — words/concepts learned through stories grow into the world
- **Maker Meadow** — later creative/drawing/story-making experiences
- **Discovery Hill** — later nature/science/numbers exploration

Extra destinations, mini-games, accounts, subscriptions and social features are deferred until Episode 001 proves the founding loop.

## Experience direction

Willow's World should feel like entering a distinctive illustrated children's universe, not opening an educational SaaS dashboard. Warm tactile storybook art, authored typography, layered scenery, gentle motion and expressive sound should make the experience feel crafted rather than generated.

## Parent/privacy principles

- parent-controlled publishing; off by default
- no automatic upload of child recordings merely because they were imported
- no child DMs/public social graph/location tracking/behavioural advertising
- local-first progress where practical
- no AI voice clone silently replacing the real child performance
- explicit rights metadata for every story/content source

## P0 definition of done

Episode 001 must demonstrate end-to-end:

1. a real recording enters the production workflow;
2. a timed/reviewable performance exists;
3. canonical illustrated scenes exist;
4. Story Woods is genuinely playable with synchronized voice/words/scenes;
5. completion changes Willow's World;
6. landscape and vertical videos render from the same performance;
7. parent preview/approval exists;
8. web preview is deployed from GitHub to the clean `willows-world-app` Vercel project;
9. mobile/web interaction and accessibility are verified;
10. restarting preserves progress.

Until this passes, new work must materially improve **Record → Transform → Watch/Play → Share**.
