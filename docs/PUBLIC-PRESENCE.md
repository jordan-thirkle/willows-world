# Willow's World — Public Presence Strategy

## Role of the public web

The public website is the discoverable front door and publishing surface for Willow's World. It is not a mirror of private family state and it is not a marketing wrapper around a generic dashboard.

## Domain direction

Use a By JTT-owned production domain/subdomain once release infrastructure is ready. The existing Vercel project URL remains useful as deployment plumbing/preview identity, not the long-term brand destination.

The exact production hostname is a deployment decision and should not be invented in documentation before DNS/project ownership is verified.

## Public route families

Keep the initial information architecture small:

- product landing/home;
- how the Record → Transform → Watch / Play → Share experience works;
- parent privacy/safety and support information;
- approved publication-safe episode/editorial pages when rights permit;
- app download/store routes once listings exist.

Do not create hundreds of thin keyword pages.

## Technical discovery baseline

Every indexable route should have intentional server-rendered/indexable content, title/description, canonical URL and share metadata. Maintain sitemap and robots behavior deliberately. Add structured data only when a schema truthfully matches visible page content.

## By JTT relationship

By JTT should establish maker/provenance trust and route relevant audience to Willow's World. Willow's World should retain its own child/family-facing identity rather than feeling like a generic page inside a developer portfolio.

## Media distribution

The approved canonical episode revision is the source of truth for landscape and vertical media. Public metadata may be adapted per destination, but episode facts, revision identity and rights/approval state remain traceable.

## X.com

Use X for authored build/product storytelling: why the product exists, craft/process, safe product milestones, public artwork/episodes and launch updates. Do not expose private childhood details simply because they improve engagement.

## Measurement

Measure public acquisition and product conversion with privacy-safe analytics. Useful outcomes include qualified landing visits, store/download intent, parent production-loop activation and successful episode creation. Child time-in-app and compulsive engagement are explicitly not growth objectives.
